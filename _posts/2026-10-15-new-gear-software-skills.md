So I've been recently learning [DaVinci Resolve Studio][dvr-studio], assisted with my [logickeyboard TITAN Keyboard][logickb].

Last month I picked up a [DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro Adventure Combo][pbtech-action5pro], along with [additional filters][1], a [hard case][2], some other accessories like tripod stands and a [magnetic mount][3]. 

### DJI Osmo Action (Gen 1)

- **Limited to 8-Bit Color Gamut**
- **Washed-out low light** - Video gets very blurry and grainy as soon as the sun goes down.
- **Basic slow motion** - Maxes out at standard 4K at 60 frames per second.
- **Short battery** - Lasts around 1 to 1.5 hours before needing a swap.
- **Basic stabilization** - Smoothes out bumps but cannot keep the video level if the camera tilts heavily.

### DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro

- **Full 10-Bit Color Gamut Support**
- **Excellent low light** - Uses a much larger sensor and AI processing to shoot clean video at night.
- **Ultra-smooth slow motion** - Shoots at 4K at 120 frames per second for ultra-fluid slow-mo.
- **Massive battery** - Lasts up to 4 hours on a single charge.
- **Horizon locking** - Keeps the footage perfectly level even if the camera flips completely upside down.
